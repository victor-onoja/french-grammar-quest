Privacy Policy

French Galaxy Quest · Last updated 4 September 2026

French Galaxy Quest is a free French grammar and vocabulary game. This policy explains exactly what it collects, why, and how to get rid of it. It is short because the game does very little with your data.

You can use it without giving us anything

If you never sign in, no personal information leaves your device. Your progress is stored only in your own browser, and we cannot see it.

What we collect if you sign in

Signing in exists for one reason: so your progress survives when your browser clears its storage, and so it follows you to another device.

  • Your email address and an account identifier. If you use Google, we receive your email address and a Google account ID. We request only basic profile scopes and never gain access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.
  • Your game progress. Which sectors you have unlocked, your XP, your star ratings, which vocabulary docks you have completed, and the words you answered incorrectly so the game can ask them again.

That is the whole list. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no profiling, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Where it is stored

Your progress is always kept in your browser's local storage. If you are signed in, a copy is also stored in a hosted database so it can be restored later.

Cookies and similar storage

No advertising or analytics cookies are used. The game stores your progress in local storage, and if you sign in it also stores a session token so you stay signed in. That is all.

Fonts are served from this site rather than from a third party, so loading a page sends your IP address to nobody but the host.

Deleting your data

Email hello@victoronojaodoh.com and ask, and your account and saved progress will be deleted. You do not have to give a reason. You can also clear your browser's site data at any time to remove the local copy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data held about you, and to object to its processing. Use the address above and we will action it.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and accounts should not be created by them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
